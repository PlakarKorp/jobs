## About Plakar & The Job

Plakar is the **open resilience-as-code platform** for enterprises preparing for inevitable breach. Built around **Kloset** (an immutable, encrypted deduplication engine) and a Kubernetes-native control plane, Plakar makes complete cyber-resilience declarative, affordable, and yours to control. No appliance. No proprietary format. No vendor-mediated recovery path.

We raised a **$3M pre-seed** led by [Seedcamp](https://seedcamp.com), with Kima Ventures, HelloWorld, and angels including **Olivier Pomel** (Datadog), **Solomon Hykes** (Docker), and **Alexandre Yazdi** (Voodoo).

Our open-source project is live and adoption is growing bottom-up inside real infrastructure teams. Now we need our first **Sales / Solutions Engineer** to own the technical side of every enterprise conversation: the person who turns "I tried it on my cluster" into a production deployment and a signed contract. This is a founding technical-sales role. You will build the playbook, not inherit it.

---

## The Role & What You'll Be Doing

You are the technical authority in the room. You partner with the founders and sales on every enterprise opportunity, own the proof, and make the buyer's engineers your champions.

**Technical Discovery & Pre-Sales**  
Lead technical discovery with DevOps, SRE, platform, and security teams. Map their backup and recovery reality (RTO/RPO targets, ransomware exposure, compliance scope, existing Rubrik/Veeam/Cohesity/Commvault footprint) to what Plakar does, and know exactly when we're the right fit.

**Demos & Proofs of Value**  
Design and deliver demos that land with practitioners, then run hands-on POVs where the prospect deploys Plakar against their own workloads: Kubernetes, databases, object storage, VMs. Define success criteria up front and drive to a clear technical win.

**Solution Architecture**  
Design reference architectures and deployment plans for real enterprise environments. Own RFP/RFI responses, security questionnaires, and the architecture diagrams that get Plakar through procurement and security review.

**Competitive & Positioning**  
Be the technical answer to "why not just use Rubrik?" Turn Plakar's open, no-lock-in, no-appliance model into a concrete architectural and economic argument the buyer's own team can defend internally.

**Product Feedback & Enablement**  
You sit closest to the buyer's technical objections. Feed them back to product and engineering as a structured signal. Build the demo environments, POV playbooks, and reference material the next SE hires will run on.

- You shipped when it mattered. A working POV this week beats a perfect deck next quarter.
