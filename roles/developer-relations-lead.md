## About Plakar & The Job

Plakar is the **open resilience-as-code platform** for enterprises preparing for inevitable breach. Built around **Kloset** — an immutable, encrypted deduplication engine — and a Kubernetes-native control plane, Plakar makes complete cyber-resilience declarative, affordable, and yours to control. No appliance. No proprietary format. No vendor-mediated recovery path.

We raised a **$3M pre-seed** led by [Seedcamp](https://seedcamp.com), with Kima Ventures, HelloWorld, and angels including **Olivier Pomel** (Datadog), **Solomon Hykes** (Docker), and **Alexandre Yazdi** (Voodoo).

We are building from scratch in the open, and we need our first Developer Relations hire to help us grow the global DevOps and SRE community around Plakar. The open-source repo is live, early users are coming in, and the community is yours to shape.

---

## The Role & What You'll Be Doing

You are Plakar's bridge to the DevOps/SRE world. You own the community, the content, and the feedback loop back to product — end to end.

**Community & Advocacy**  
Run Plakar's GitHub community, build and moderate the Discord, and represent us at KubeCon, SREcon, HashiConf, and FOSDEM with talks and workshops that make practitioners want to try Plakar the same day.

**Open-Source Adoption**  
Own the first-run experience — README to working backup in under 10 minutes. Manage the contributor funnel from good-first-issues to core maintainers. Ship integrations where DevOps/SRE teams already live: Kubernetes, GitHub Actions, Terraform, Prometheus.

**Technical Content**  
2 high-quality technical posts per month (architecture deep dives, benchmarks, comparison guides). A demo video library. A developer newsletter. Documentation that does not embarrass the engineering team.

**Product Feedback**  
Turn community signals into a monthly structured report for the founding team. Run beta programs for new features. Be the voice of the developer inside Plakar.

## What We Need To See

**Non-negotiables**
- You can set up a Plakar backup pipeline yourself. Technical depth is table stakes with this audience — you cannot fake it with DevOps/SREs.
- Native or C2 English. All content and community communications are in English.
- You know how open-source communities actually work — contributor norms, governance, the unwritten rules.
- You speak the language: RTO, RPO, immutable storage, deduplication, GitOps, zero-trust.

**Strong signals**
- You have grown a technical community from the ground up, not just managed an established one.
- You have content in the wild — posts, talks, demos — that demonstrably moved a metric.
- You shipped when it mattered. A good tutorial today beats a perfect one next quarter.
