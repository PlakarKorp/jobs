# Chief of Staff

Location: Europe (Remote) · Type: Full-time · Stage: Pre-Series A

Contact: founders@plakar.io

## About Plakar & The Job

Data loss, ransomware, and multi-cloud complexity are at an all-time high, yet the market completely lacks a modern open-source standard for data resilience. While modern infrastructure stacks have evolved rapidly to power our industry, the underlying backup and archival layers are still trapped in legacy paradigms from a different era. Nobody has made a definitive play to establish an open-source standard for this essential pillar of modern tech infrastructure.

At Plakar, we are changing that. We are building the next big thing in data resiliency: a modular, zero-knowledge storage and backup ecosystem built around Kloset (an immutable, encrypted deduplication engine) and designed specifically for the modern infrastructure stacks used across the industry today.

We raised a $3M pre-seed led by Seedcamp, with Kima Ventures, HelloWorld, and angels including Olivier Pomel (Datadog), Solomon Hykes (Docker), and Alexandre Yazdi (Voodoo). We are scaling fast to own this critical market standard, and we need a high-velocity Chief of Staff to join us as our operational engine and help drive Plakar to global execution.

## The Role & What You'll Be Doing

You are the operational and strategic backbone of Plakar. Your role is to shield the founders from administrative, financial, and organizational complexity so they can remain entirely focused on product and engineering velocity. You will turn fast-paced strategic decisions into scalable execution, optimize our business workflows, and ensure Plakar remains investment-ready and fully compliant for international enterprise clients.

### Governance, Automation & Business Operations

- **Executive Voice and Synthesis:** Serve as the primary written voice of the executive office. You have a unique ability to absorb fast-paced verbal inputs or raw strategic brain-dumps from the founders and instantly translate them into clean, highly structured, actionable written assets.
    
- **Source of Truths Ownership:** Build, structure, and continuously maintain the internal operations section of our knowledge base, known as the "Source of Truths". For the first 6 months, your scope is strictly limited to standardizing internal workflows: the recruitment and onboarding pipeline, team rituals, expense tracking rules, and general administrative protocols, ensuring they are audited and updated weekly.
    
- **Process Automation (BizOps):** Proactively identify operational bottlenecks. Map internal workflows and deploy automation tools (such as Zapier, Make, or custom scripts) to eliminate repetitive manual overhead and maximize overall team velocity.
    
- **Revenue and Delivery Operations:** Optimize the customer delivery pipeline. You will set up and manage automated workflows for tracking client onboarding, invoicing, billing, and cash collection to ensure a seamless experience for Plakar Enterprise clients.
    

### Strategy & Investor Relations

- **Non-Dilutive Funding:** Own and execute the strategy to secure 2.2M€+ in non-dilutive funding from the BPI, either directly or by steering external experts.
    
- **Investor Relations:** Generate high-quality quarterly reports for our investors and manage the comprehensive documentation package for board meetings.
    
- **Fundraising Readiness:** Build and permanently maintain a professional, audit-ready Data Room to support our upcoming Series A efforts.
    

### Administration, Financial Control & Compliance

- **Financial Interface:** Serve as the primary point of contact for external accounting teams to ensure flawless, zero-delay monthly and yearly closures.
    
- **Expense Analytics:** Design and monitor a granular analytical expense plan to track our financial runway and burn rate with absolute precision.
    
- **Certification Closer:** Take the lead on finalized compliance initiatives. Our SOC2 and ISO 27001 readiness roadmap is already 80% complete: your job is to coordinate the final audit phases and secure the certifications within 3 months by pragmatically leveraging existing technical resources.
    
- **Data Privacy:** Keep the company's global operations and the Plakar Provider Protocol aligned with international data protection standards (GDPR).
    

### Talent & Culture Management

- **A-Player Recruitment:** Structure and track a world-class recruitment pipeline based on data-driven scorecard methodologies to attract elite talent.
    
- **Onboarding Execution:** Design an exceptional onboarding framework that ensures every new hire adopts Plakar's core culture (Radical Candor, Velocity-Driven, Customer Obsession) within their first 30 days.
    

## What We Need To See

- **Verbal-to-Written Conversion:** Master-level ability to synthesize spoken words or raw conversations into clear protocols, corporate playbooks, and investor decks without requiring constant oversight.
    
- **Linguistic Mastery:** Native or C2 English. All internal playbooks, investor reports, and board communications are conducted in English.
    
- **BizOps Tooling Expertise:** Hands-on experience with workflow automation platforms and modern financial SaaS billing architectures (such as Stripe, HubSpot, or specialized invoicing tools).
    
- **Multicultural Mindset:** Deep familiarity with high-velocity international tech environments, combined with the ability to orchestrate European or French local administration (BPI, legal counsel, accountants), whether you speak French yourself or manage local partners to execute it.
    

### Strong signals

- You have scaled a tech startup's operations or worked closely with executive leadership in a fast-growing environment.
    
- You have built automated client onboarding, billing, and cash collection frameworks from scratch.
    
- You shipped when it mattered: a functional, automated operational fix today beats a perfect process next quarter.
